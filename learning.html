The bigger picture

Your growth roadmap

A clear next step at every stage.
  1. Discover: Complete the foundation lessons and Discover Checklist.
  2. Build: Build your approach through learning, workshops and practice.
  3. Achieve: The current platform requires a first Profit Split to enter this stage.
  4. Legacy: The current platform links cumulative 10% X Mode Profit with a Fund Add-on.

Based on the current learning page. Official platform and provider rules apply.

What comes after

1Stage 1

Discover

This is where you begin. Through videos and documents, learn how the platform and the trading process work, along with the basic rules, and build a sound view of trading.

E-Learning

A full library of video lessons, to learn wherever you are.

What you will learn

  • Understand the platform and the trading process
  • Learn the basic rules and how contracts work
  • Build a sound approach to trading
2Stage 2

Build

Start studying trading in a structured way, make good use of the available trading tools, and build a trading plan, risk management and discipline. This is also the stage where you begin turning knowledge into practical trading ability.

Workshop

Coach-led online workshops with live-trade walkthroughs and Q&A.

What you will learn

  • Study trading lessons in a structured way
  • Make good use of trading tools
  • Build a trading plan, risk management and discipline
🔒 How to enter Build?
Complete every Discover Checklist

Once the fundamentals are confirmed, you can move on to Build.

3Stage 3

Achieve

Achieve is about more than making a profit. A single win can come from the market offering an opportunity, but what matters is managing capital and controlling risk consistently, and producing steady results. That is real trading ability.

Private Event

An invitation to offline private events, alongside traders who have earned profits.

What you will learn

  • Manage capital consistently
  • Keep risk under steady control
  • Produce steady results, not one-off gains
🔒 How to enter Achieve?
Complete your first Profit Split

It shows you have applied what you learned in live trading and begun to produce real results.

4Stage 4

Legacy

Legacy is the goal most worth pursuing for every trader. Each Fund Add-on shows your ability has been proven once again. Real Legacy is not about how much you make at once, but about continually pushing past your limits and becoming a better trader.

Certified Trader

Earn Certified Trader recognition and your own Trader Career Profile.

What you will learn

  • Keep pushing yourself to become a better trader
  • Every Fund Add-on is proof of your ability
  • Have a Trader Career Profile of your own
🔒 How to enter Legacy?
Reach 10% cumulative Profit on X Mode

You then receive a Fund Add-on that increases your account funds by 20%. For example, USD25,000 is upgraded to USD30,000.
